MEET xBAKE

Quality control,
fresh from the browser.

xBAKE is an open-source vision system that detects, classifies, and rejects defective baked goods on a conveyor.

01DetectFind each product in the camera frame.
02ClassifyEvaluate color and shape with transparent rules.
03ActReject defects and keep an inspection record.

This standalone demo uses generated images, illustrative measurements, and simulated controls. It does not run OpenCV or ONNX, access a camera, operate hardware, or connect to a production database. The 128 starting records are sample history; new records come from the animated simulation. Rule scores are illustrative, not measured model accuracy.
